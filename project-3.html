Business challenge

Teams maintained requirements in separate documents, email chains, and spreadsheets. Version confusion, duplicate requirements, missing approvals, and weak change impact analysis created delivery risk.

Solution approach

Defined a centralized portal for requirement lifecycle management, approval workflow, change-control intake, impact analysis, traceability, UAT status, and release-readiness dashboards.
