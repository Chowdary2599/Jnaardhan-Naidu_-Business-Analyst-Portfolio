Business challenge

Claims teams relied on manual validation, disconnected systems, and scattered UAT evidence. Duplicate submissions and incomplete claims increased rework and slowed adjudication.

Solution approach

Defined a centralized claims platform with automated field validation, duplicate detection, exception work queues, UAT tracking, dashboards, access controls, and audit logging.
