Business challenge

Risk teams manually consolidated exception, settlement, SLA, and control data from multiple spreadsheets. Reporting was slow, inconsistent, and difficult to audit.

Solution approach

Defined a risk dashboard that consolidates source data, calculates KRIs, highlights trends and control breaks, tracks remediation, and generates AI-assisted management summaries.
