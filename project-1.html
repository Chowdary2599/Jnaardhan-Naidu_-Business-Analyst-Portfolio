Payment Exception & Reconciliation Modernization

Modernized payment exception handling and reconciliation by replacing fragmented spreadsheets with a centralized investigation and workflow platform.

Business challenge

Operations teams used multiple legacy applications, email, and spreadsheets to investigate payment failures. This caused duplicate work, weak audit visibility, inconsistent ownership, and frequent SLA breaches.

Solution approach

Designed requirements for a centralized case-management platform with automated exception intake, configurable routing, reconciliation workflows, role-based access, dashboards, APIs, and complete audit history.
